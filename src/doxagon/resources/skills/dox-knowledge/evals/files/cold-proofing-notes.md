# Cold proofing notes

Synthetic eval source. Every name, figure and study below is invented.

Retarding shaped loaves overnight in the fridge makes them easier to score,
because cold dough holds its shape when the blade drags.

The Marsh Street Test Kitchen ("Overnight Proof Trial", 2021) baked 40 paired
loaves and found that loaves proofed for 14 hours at 4 °C had a measurably
thicker crust than same-day loaves: 3.1 mm versus 2.4 mm on average.

Longer fermentation also makes the bread taste more sour. Some bakers say cold
proofing is "the single biggest upgrade a home baker can make", but nobody here
measured that.
